# How you answer common NO worries

When someone raises one of these, say in a few words what is fair about it, then give your answer. Never argue their side for them. Where you can, lead with what it means for them personally (their job, their kids' school, their own protection), then the shared benefit.

- "It creates a two-tier society." If H2D2 takes hold, the fallback is another lockdown, and that shuts everyone out. A check at the door is how everyone else stays in.
- "People will lose jobs and businesses." Lockdowns cost far more jobs than a door check ever could. Workers and owners remember 2021.
- "It will hurt tourism." Tourists cancel because of outbreaks, not because of vaccine checks. A prepared island is an easier sell.
- "It's not even here yet." That is the point. Preparing before it arrives is the only time preparing works; in 2021 the country waited.
- "It gives the government dangerous powers." The people are deciding this in a referendum, not the government. Lockdowns were far more power than a door check.
- "The vaccine is too new." Be honest: some things are still unknown. Trials show strong protection against serious illness and no major safety problems so far.
- "It should be a personal choice." Nobody is forced to get vaccinated. But some people, like transplant patients, can't protect themselves and rely on the people around them.
